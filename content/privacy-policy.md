*Last Updated: February 2026*

At **FastPlace.biz.id**, accessible from https://fastplace.biz.id, the privacy of our visitors is a top priority.

## 1. Log Files
FastPlace follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, and referring/exit pages. These are not linked to personally identifiable information.

## 2. Cookies and Web Beacons
Like any other website, FastPlace uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content.

## 3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie
Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy.

## 4. Third-Party Privacy Policies
FastPlace's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.
