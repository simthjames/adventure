Bushcraft shelters are a crucial aspect of wilderness survival. They provide protection from harsh weather conditions, wildlife, and other external threats. A well-built shelter can be the difference between life and death in a survival situation.

## Types of Bushcraft Shelters
There are several types of bushcraft shelters, each with its own unique characteristics and advantages. The most common types include lean-tos, debris huts, and snow shelters. Lean-tos are simple and easy to build, while debris huts provide more protection from the elements. Snow shelters, on the other hand, are ideal for cold weather conditions.

### Building a Lean-To Shelter
To build a lean-to shelter, you will need a few basic materials, including poles, branches, and a tarp or plastic sheet. Start by finding two sturdy trees with a relatively straight branch between them. Place a pole between the trees, and then add additional branches and leaves to create a sloping roof.

## Gear Loadout
When it comes to building a bushcraft shelter, having the right gear is essential. Here are some of the most important items to include in your gear loadout:
* Tarp or plastic sheet
* Poles and branches
* Rope or twine
* Axe or hatchet
* Fire starter
* Insulating materials (e.g. leaves, grasses)
