## Field Execution
In the field, the Counterpart excels as a utilitarian tool. The 3.38" drop point blade is made from 420J1 stainless steel, offering a balance of edge retention and corrosion resistance. The ergonomic handle, constructed from GRN, fits comfortably in the hand, allowing for precise control over the blade. Whether you're cutting kindling or slicing through cordage, the Counterpart proves itself to be a capable companion.
